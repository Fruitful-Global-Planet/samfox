Heart of Her Hand

A cover note on your work, your name, and what belongs to you.

Samantha — every mark in the Sam Fox™ system carries your hand. The ink outline that never smooths itself into something vector-perfect. The fox head that stands in for the "o" in your own name. The six moves that turn a blank page into a creature with a heart, signed bottom-right, always yours.

Since September 2016, working freelance as SAM FOX ILLUSTRATES out of Pretoria, that hand has drawn its way through The Actuate Group, Lightspot & West Pack, Homemart Toybox, the 2023 Bold Condoms rebrand, and — since 2018 — the whole of Banimal™ — Kind Creatures. A BA in Visual Communication from The Open Window, 2010, and everything since has been built on it. It is still your hand. It is still yours.

What this note says plainly

This exists to put in writing what has sometimes only been implied: the copyright in your original illustration work — the Sam Fox™ wordmark, the fox-head icon, the Banimal™ character work, the Fruitful Portal illustrations, and everything drawn under the Sam Fox™ name — is intended to be documented and filed in your own legal name and personal identity. Not in the name of Fruitful Shops (Pty) Ltd.

Fruitful Shops is the commercial vehicle — the one that licenses the work, builds a business around it, carries it out of the bush and into the world. It is not, and is not intended to become, the owner of the work itself. The hand that drew it keeps it.

That intent needs to be formalized properly — by an IP attorney, filed correctly in each jurisdiction where it matters — before it carries full legal weight on its own. This document is the first step: your acknowledgment in writing, and everything a filing will need, gathered in one place.
